## Phase Goal

**As a** person in Recife who wants to visit It's Garlic, **I want to** find the store's real address,
how it serves (balcão, delivery, take away), the honest state of its opening hours, and a working
directions link, **so that** I can get there without guessing.

*(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `**Goal**` line — not invented here. This plan delivers the
location half of that goal; plan 02-01 delivered the hero and CTA half.)*

<objective>
Add the location section: the full address from the validated store record, the service modalities as
a row that survives the array changing, the hours block that tells the truth about an unconfirmed
schedule instead of inventing one, and the one outbound link in this whole phase that is actually
confirmed and live.

Purpose: this is the second half of the ROADMAP's launch-gate slice, and it is where the project's
content-integrity rule gets its real test. The only hours references that exist are two Instagram
stories 2.5 to 4.8 years old that contradict each other. The section has to be genuinely useful
without pretending to know something it does not.

Output: `src/components/home/Location.tsx`, its rendered-output branch tests, the location copy,
the page wiring, and the location guards added to the phase's regression suite.
</objective>
