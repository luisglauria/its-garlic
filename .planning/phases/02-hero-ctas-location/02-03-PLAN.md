## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor who has just met the brand in the hero, **I want to** read what It's Garlic actually
is and get the obvious practical questions answered on the spot, **so that** I can decide to order
or to visit without leaving the page to find out.

*(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `**Goal**` line and CONTEXT.md D-01, which widens the phase's
sections to five — not invented here.)*

<objective>
Close the phase by filling the two remaining sections the Phase 1 content skeleton assigned to it:
the brand story and the FAQ. Both are final copy this phase, both are written from confirmed facts
only, and the FAQ uses the browser's own disclosure element so the entire homepage still ships with
zero client-side JavaScript.

Purpose: the hero says what the brand is in five words; this is where a visitor who wants a little
more gets it, and where the practical questions that would otherwise send them to Instagram get
answered. It is also the phase's largest block of prose, which makes it the place where an invented
fact is most likely to slip in — so this plan ends with a mechanical content-integrity gate over the
whole copy module, not just a review.

Output: `BrandStory.tsx`, `Faq.tsx`, the brand-story and FAQ copy, their rendered-output tests, the
content-integrity gate, and the completed five-section composition.
</objective>
