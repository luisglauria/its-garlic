## Phase Goal

**As a** hungry person in Recife opening the It's Garlic site on my phone, **I want to** understand in
seconds that this is "Mais que um pão de alho!" and act on the order and directions CTAs right
there, **so that** I can order or get to the store without hunting for anything.

*(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `**Goal**` line — not invented here. The full phase goal also
covers the location/hours slice, delivered by plan 02-02, and the brand-story/FAQ sections,
delivered by plan 02-03.)*

<objective>
Land the above-the-fold half of the homepage: the brand concept as real text on the brand's own
surface, a CLS-safe hero image slot carrying the labelled provisional illustration, and the CTA row
with the three locked labels — where the two unconfirmed order destinations render as honest,
inert, clearly-labelled "em breve" buttons instead of live links to a destination that does not
exist yet.

Purpose: this is the phase's tracer. It proves the whole chain this phase depends on — typed copy
module → section component → page composition → prerendered static HTML → the Phase 1
repository/integration seams — on one thin path, before plans 02-02 and 02-03 expand horizontally
across the remaining three sections. If the composition or the confirmed-gate shape is wrong, it is
wrong after one commit, not after five sections are already built on it.

Output: `src/content/home-copy.ts`, `public/brand/hero-illustration.svg`, four `home/` components,
two test files, the rewritten home composition, and the image-format config.
</objective>
